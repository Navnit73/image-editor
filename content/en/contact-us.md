# Contact PhotoResizer AI

Thank you for using **PhotoResizer AI**, your free, browser-based online AI image editor and photo resizer. Whether you have questions about biometric passport photos, feature suggestions, or privacy commitments, we are eager to hear from you.

### Support & Inquiries
- **General Support**: support@photoresizerai.com
- **Privacy & GDPR Officers**: privacy@photoresizerai.com
- **Response Time**: 24–48 business hours

### Privacy Guarantee Notice
Please note that because PhotoResizer AI operates **100% client-side** using HTML5 Canvas and WebGL APIs, our support team has **zero access** to your uploaded photos, edited files, or local storage. You never need to worry about your sensitive images being seen by anyone.
