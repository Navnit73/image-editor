# Frequently Asked Questions

### General & Pricing
1. **Is PhotoResizer AI completely free to use?**  
   Yes. All features — including image resizing, cropping, compression, format conversion (PNG to JPG), and passport photo generation — are 100% free without watermarks or usage limits.

2. **Do I need to register or create an account?**  
   No. You can launch the studio and edit photos instantly without creating an account or providing an email address.

---

### Privacy & Data Security
3. **Are my photos uploaded to any server?**  
   Never. PhotoResizer AI processes every pixel inside your web browser memory (RAM) using HTML5 Canvas and WebGL technology. Your files never leave your computer or phone.

4. **Is PhotoResizer AI compliant with GDPR and CCPA regulations?**  
   Yes. Because we do not upload, store, or transmit your images or personal data, privacy compliance is guaranteed by design.

---

### Tools & Technical Specifications
5. **How do I crop images for Instagram or LinkedIn?**  
   Select the **Image Cropper** tool, pick a preset aspect ratio (1:1 Square, 4:5 Portrait, or 16:9 Landscape), adjust the crop box, and click export.

6. **Can I convert PNG images to JPG or WebP?**  
   Yes. When exporting your edited image, select your preferred output format (PNG, JPG, or WebP) and quality percentage.

7. **How does the Passport Photo Resizer work?**  
   Choose your document standard (US 2x2 inches, EU Schengen 35x45mm, or Brazil 3x4cm). Alignment guides for eye height and facial width overlay your canvas to ensure official compliance.
